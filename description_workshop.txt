[h1] Lock Hotbar Scroll Wheel [/h1]
A mod that lets you disable the scrolling on the hotbar with your mouse wheel.

[h1] Key Features [/h1]
[b] Toggle: [/b] Adds a toggle to the left of the player's inventory that can enable or disable scrolling the hotbar with your mouse wheel.

[b] Hotkey: [/b] Adds a bindable hotkey
[list]

    [*][b] Toggle Scroll Wheel Lock: [/b] Allows the toggling of the hotbar scroll wheel lock.
[/list]

[b] Config: [/b] 
[list]
    [*][b] Lock is affected during normal play: [/b] The hotbar scroll wheel lock will be active during non-bosses and non-events (Enabled by default)

    [*][b] Lock is affected during events: [/b] The hotbar scroll wheel lock will be active during events (Enabled by default)

    [*][b] Lock is affected during boss battles: [/b] The hotbar scroll wheel lock will be active during boss battles (Enabled by default)
[/list]

[h1] Open Source [/h1] 
The source code is fully viewable on GitHub or by extracting the mod.

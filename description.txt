A mod that disables scrolling on the hotbar with your mouse wheel. 

Adds a toggle that can enable or disable the hotbar scrolling or can be bind through a hotkey. Through the config you can enbable/disable if the locking works during normal play, events, boss battles. 

Should at least work with vanilla bosses/events however it may not work properly if mods added in events/bosses in a non-conventional way, if so you can list it inside the discussion on the mod page through steam.

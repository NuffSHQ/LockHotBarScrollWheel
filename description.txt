[c/FFFF00:=== Lock Hotbar Scroll Wheel ===]
A mod that lets you disable the scrolling on the hotbar with your mouse wheel.

[c/ffeb3b:Key Features]
    [c/ffeb3b:Toggle:]  Adds a toggle to the left of the player's inventory that can enable or disable scrolling the hotbar with your mouse wheel.

    [c/ffeb3b:Hotkey:]  Adds a bindable hotkey
        [c/ffeb3b:Toggle Scroll Wheel Lock:] Allows the toggling of the hotbar scroll wheel lock.

    [c/ffeb3b:Config:] 
        [c/ffeb3b:Lock is affected during normal play:] 
            The hotbar scroll wheel lock will be active during non-bosses and non-events
        [c/ffeb3b:Lock is affected during events:]
            The hotbar scroll wheel lock will be active during events
        [c/ffeb3b:Lock is affected during boss battles:]
            The hotbar scroll wheel lock will be active during boss battles

    [c/ffeb3b:Open Source]
        The source code is fully viewable on GitHub or by extracting the mod.
